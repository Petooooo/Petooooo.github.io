## Purpose

This note defines the shape of future paper reviews. The goal is not to write academic summaries for their own sake. A useful review should help answer whether an idea, method, evaluation approach, or implementation detail is worth revisiting during engineering work.

## Review Shape

- Problem: what the paper is trying to solve.
- Core idea: the smallest useful explanation of the contribution.
- Implementation details: algorithms, systems design, data structures, architecture, workload, or evaluation setup.
- Practical observations: what might transfer to infrastructure, software, embedded systems, or AI infrastructure work.
- Limitations: assumptions, missing measurements, weak comparisons, or deployment concerns.
- References: papers, systems, tools, or datasets that should be followed later.

## Scope

Paper reviews in this archive should stay within computer science, electrical/electronic engineering, systems engineering, AI infrastructure, software engineering, algorithms, networking, architecture, and embedded or system-level engineering.

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Confirm the paper title, authors, venue, and year.
- [ ] Keep claims tied to the paper, not memory.
- [ ] Separate paper findings from personal interpretation.
- [ ] Note implementation details that could be reproduced or adapted.
- [ ] Record limitations and follow-up papers.
